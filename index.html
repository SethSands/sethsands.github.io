How to make Chocolate Fudge

Ingredients:

  • Dark Chocolate (500g)
  • Condensed Milk (400g)
  • Butter (40g) -- Unsalted is best, but I usually use salted since it's easier to get
  • [Optional] Walnuts -- for texture, flavour and a bit of cooking assistance, though I myself don't much prefer it.
  • [Optional] Cooking Paper + Container -- only really needed if you don't want to let the fudge sit in the pan, wihch is generally a good idea, but it doesn't really affect the end result. Youd do need this if you keep this in the fridge though.

Prep

  • Crush or chop the dark chocolate, so it melts while cooking.
  • Do the same for walnutes, if you wish to add them.
  • If you're using the container, oil the cooking paper just a little after stuffing it into the container.

Method

  1. Get a wide pan, put it on the stove, and pour all your condensed milk into it.
  2. Add all the crushed/chopped dark chocolate and stir.
  3. Add the butter and continue to stir. You can also add the walnuts at this stage.
  4. Keep stirring until it starts getting thick. The walnuts should start adding a small amount of course-ish texture thanks to the oils they release.
  5. Once the fudge starts to move less fluidly and you get that sweet chocolate smell you can turn off the gas and pour it into the container, or keep it in the pan, and let it cool for a few hours.
You're Done!
